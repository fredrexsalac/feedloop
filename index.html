About FeedLoop

FeedLoop is a comprehensive feedback management system designed specifically for Philippine educational institutions.

Built with modern web technologies and following international security standards, FeedLoop provides a secure, efficient, and user-friendly platform for collecting, managing, and analyzing feedback from students, faculty, and staff.

Frequently Asked Questions

Create an account or login to your existing account, navigate to the feedback section, fill out the form with your concerns or suggestions, and submit. You'll receive a confirmation message upon successful submission.

Your feedback is linked to your user account for tracking and follow-up purposes. Only authorized administrators can access your feedback and identifying information. All feedback is treated confidentially and used solely for improvement purposes.

FeedLoop is fully compliant with the Data Privacy Act of 2012. We implement strict data protection measures including encrypted storage, limited access controls, and secure transmission protocols. Your personal information is only used for feedback processing and institutional communication.

User Accounts: Can create accounts, submit feedback, and receive confirmation messages.

Admin Accounts: Have additional privileges including viewing all feedback submissions, managing feedback status, and accessing the administrative dashboard.

Super Admin: Has the highest level of access, including the ability to manage other admin accounts and system settings.

FeedLoop offers: Local Compliance - Built for Philippine institutions with Data Privacy Act compliance, Cost-Effective - Lower costs than international platforms, Customizable - Tailored for Filipino academic culture, Responsive Support - Local technical support, and Mobile-First Design - Optimized for smartphone usage.
